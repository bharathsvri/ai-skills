At Brightline Labs last year, our payments API was the slowest service we ran, and nobody could tell me whether the old code or the database was to blame. My job was to make it fast without freezing payments work for a quarter. I could not get a full profile in time, so I decided to bet on a rebuild in Go. I wrote a design doc with the risks spelled out and ran a design review with product and finance stakeholders, who pushed back on the risk. I proposed moving one endpoint at a time behind a flag so we could roll back in seconds, and I rebuilt the hottest endpoints first. As a result, p99 latency dropped 38% across 4M requests a day, and design reviews became how my team plans every large change.
