Minimum qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
- 2 years of experience with software development in Go, C++ or Java.
- 2 years of experience building distributed systems.
- 2 years of experience with data structures or algorithms.

Preferred qualifications:
- Master's degree or PhD in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Experience with Kubernetes, containers and Google Cloud Platform.
- Experience with machine learning systems, ranking or recommendations.
- Experience improving the latency, performance and reliability of production services.
- Experience with on-call, incident response and runbooks.

About the job
Google Cloud's infrastructure teams build the compute, storage and networking that customers run their businesses on. In this role you will work on a service at the core of Google Cloud, with the chance to switch teams and projects as you and the business grow.

Responsibilities
- Write and test product or system development code in Go.
- Participate in, or lead, design reviews with peers and stakeholders.
- Do code reviews for other engineers and give feedback on testing and style.
- Triage production issues and debug them, then improve monitoring so they do not recur.
- Mentor newer engineers and contribute to documentation.
